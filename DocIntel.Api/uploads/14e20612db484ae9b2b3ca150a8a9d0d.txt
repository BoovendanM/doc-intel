Contoso Remote Work Policy (v2.3)

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible to work remotely
up to three days per week. Contractors must obtain written approval from their engagement manager.

2. Core Hours
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone.
Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

3. Equipment
Contoso provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset to every remote employee.
Employees can claim a one-time home office allowance of 500 USD for furniture such as a desk or chair.
Claims must be submitted through the Expenses portal within 60 days of purchase.

4. Security
Employees must connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems.
Company data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

5. Internet Reimbursement
Remote employees are reimbursed up to 40 USD per month for home internet costs.
